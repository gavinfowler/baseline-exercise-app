Baseline

Train offline. Your data stays yours.

A tracker for cardio and strength training that runs entirely on your device. No account, no sync, no ads, and no internet permission.

Two ways to plan

Static The plan's baseline rises. Complete the prescribed reps at a heavier weight and that becomes the new target.
Periodized The plan does not change. Beating it is recorded as a personal record, but the prescribed numbers stand for the whole program.

What else it does

  • Strength sets, supersets and circuits, with a rest timer that starts itself.
  • Cardio with duration, distance and pace — enter any two and the third works itself out.
  • Structured runs: fartlek, interval repeats, tempo blocks and pyramids, each segment with its own pace.
  • Plans built in the app, or written as JSON — the format is published so an AI tool can generate one you can import.
  • History, progress charts, personal records, and full backup and restore.

Privacy

Baseline collects nothing at all. See the privacy policy.
