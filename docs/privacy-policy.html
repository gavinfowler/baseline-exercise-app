Privacy Policy

Baseline · Last updated 15 August 2026

Baseline collects nothing. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers. Everything you record stays on your device. The released Android app does not even hold the INTERNET permission, so it is not technically capable of transmitting your data anywhere.

What the app stores

Baseline keeps your training data — exercises, workouts, sets, cardio efforts, plans, personal records and settings — in a database in the app's own private storage on your device. On Android, that area is accessible only to Baseline, and Android removes it when you uninstall the app.

What the app sends

Nothing. There is no network code in Baseline, no third-party SDK, no crash reporter and no telemetry. You can verify this yourself: the app's release build declares no internet permission, which you can inspect in the Play Store listing under App permissions, and the source is public.

Permissions, and why they exist

  • Notifications — to show the rest timer alert when you have switched away from the app. Declining only means rest alerts stay inside the app; nothing else changes.
  • Vibration — to buzz when a rest timer finishes.
  • Alarms and reminders — so a rest timer fires at the moment it is due rather than whenever the system next batches background work.
  • Run at startup — to restore a pending rest alert after the device reboots.

None of these involve the network, your location, your contacts, your camera, or your microphone. Baseline requests no such access.

Files you choose to open or save

Baseline can import a training plan from a file you select, and can export a backup of your data to a location you choose. These actions happen only when you start them, and only with the file you pick. A backup you export is an ordinary file on your device: if you later move it to a cloud drive or send it to someone, that is your decision and it leaves this policy's scope.

Copying your training out to an AI tool

Baseline can copy a summary of your recent training — sessions, sets, cardio efforts and personal records — to your clipboard, or save it as a file, so that you can paste it into an AI chat tool and ask for a new plan. The app only ever puts that text on your clipboard or into a file you name. It does not contact any AI service, and it has no connection to one.

What happens after you paste is outside Baseline and outside this policy. The tool you paste into is a separate service with its own privacy policy and its own rules about retaining and training on what you send it. If that matters to you, read those terms before pasting, and remember the export describes your training history. Nothing in Baseline requires you to use this feature.

Your data, and deleting it

Because nothing is transmitted, there is no account to close and no copy of your data for anyone to hold. You can delete individual records inside the app, and uninstalling Baseline removes the database along with it. Export a backup first if you want to keep your history.

Children

Baseline is a general-audience fitness tool. It collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Baseline ever handles data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
