## Full description (4000 max)

```
Baseline is a training log for people who want their data on their own device and nothing in the way of using it. No account, no sign-up, no sync, no ads. The Android build does not even request internet permission, so it cannot send your training anywhere.

TWO WAYS TO PLAN

Static — the plan's baseline rises. Complete the prescribed reps at a heavier weight and that weight becomes the new target. The plan carries your progress forward on its own.

Periodized — the plan does not change. Beat it and the result is saved as a personal record, but the prescribed numbers stand for the whole program.

A static baseline is tracked per plan, per exercise, per rep count, because promotion means the heaviest weight you completed at the prescribed reps. A set cut short never promotes.

STRENGTH

- Weight, reps and RPE, with warm-up sets flagged so they stay out of your numbers.
- Supersets and circuits: put two or more exercises in one block and give it a round count and its own rest.
- A rest timer that starts itself when you log a working set, with sound, vibration and a notification so it still reaches you when you switch away.

CARDIO

- Duration, distance, incline, resistance, heart rate, calories and elevation, with the fields following the activity.
- Enter any two of duration, distance and pace and the third works itself out.
- A stopwatch with start, pause and lap, saved as splits.
- Structured runs: fartlek, interval repeats, tempo blocks and pyramids, built from segments that each carry their own pace. Start from a template and change the numbers.
- No GPS, and no location permission.

PLANS

Build a plan in the app, or import one from a file. The plan format is a published JSON Schema, which makes the AI route simple: the import screen copies that schema and a ready-made prompt to your clipboard, optionally with your recent training alongside it. Paste it into any AI chat tool, save the reply, and import it. Baseline never contacts an AI service itself — the copying and pasting stays in your hands.

PROGRESS

- A full history of past sessions.
- Per-exercise charts: top set, estimated 1RM, distance and pace.
- All-time personal records.
- A starter catalog of 47 exercises, filterable by muscle group, activity and equipment, that you can add to.
- Metric or imperial, whichever you would rather read.

PERMISSIONS

Notifications, vibration and exact alarms, all so the rest timer lands on time, plus run-at-startup so a pending rest alert survives a reboot. Nothing else: no location, no camera, no microphone, no contacts.

YOUR DATA

Everything lives in a database in the app's private storage. Export a full JSON backup whenever you like and restore it on another device. Uninstalling takes the lot with it — there is no copy anywhere else, because there is nowhere else. No analytics, no crash reporting, no third-party SDKs, no in-app purchases.

Baseline is open source: https://github.com/gavinfowler/baseline-exercise-app
```
